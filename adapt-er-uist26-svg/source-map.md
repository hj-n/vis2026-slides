## Source audit details

- Table 1 selected values: GPT-5 Iteration 1, 5.84 ± 1.32% unfamiliar words; 211.8 ± 3.3s; $0.16/article. GPT-4.1 Iteration 1, 11.12 ± 6.92%; 3.4 ± 0.6s; <$0.01/article. GPT-4.1 Iteration 3, 5.71 ± 1.73%; 4.8 ± 0.6s; $0.02/article; BERTScore 0.9663; human score 4.67/5.
- Fig. 4 gives p=.00418 for reading speed (body rounds to .0042) and p=.0178 for tooltip use. We use the figure precision consistently. No mean reading-speed or tooltip count is inferred from point locations.
- Table 2 gives participant-level 5-point self-reports; the paper interprets them descriptively. We do not add a significance test.
- The 79.17% / 95.83% lightweight reading-verification result is omitted. It is not a comprehension-test result.
- Fig. 1 shows milestone preserved and interdisciplinary rewritten; Fig. 3 also explicitly shows convened→gathered. Their example text is illustrative (UIST 2084), not a claim about an actual conference.
- Personal remote-presentation/faculty-interview context comes only from the user-supplied plan. We do not invent an interview location or date.
